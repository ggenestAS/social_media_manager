## Hooks that fit the brand

- **The exam-room truth:** no tabs, no chatbot, no upload button; you stay.
- **Recognizing ≠ knowing:** you studied, then your mind went blank.
- **The abandoned plan:** honest question — how many revision plans have you
  abandoned?
- **They're right:** quote a real headline about AI hollowing out students,
  agree with it, then turn: "so we built the other kind."
- **Same technology, opposite goal.**

## Press mechanic rules

1. Headline = exact published wording, masthead, date. Verify the article
   exists and says that; keep the link in `post.md`.
2. Agree first, then turn. Never mock the outlet or the students.
3. One headline per board (splits, reels); the stack of four is only for the
   carousel hook card.
4. Text masthead by default; a real logo only with sign-off.
5. Numbers quoted from the press (e.g. « 90 % des élèves de seconde ») must
   be attributable to the cited article — otherwise cut the number.
