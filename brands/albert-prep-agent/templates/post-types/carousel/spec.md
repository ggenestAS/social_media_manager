# Carousel — "They're right."

**Format:** 1080 × 1350 px (4:5) · **4 cards** · one board per card in
`source.html`, labelled `Carousel · 1/4` … `4/4`.
**Export:** `npm run html:to-image -- source.html --all --out export/`

## Concept

Agree with the press, then turn. The swipe is the argument: a stack of real
headlines (card 1), the problem in one sentence (card 2), the product as the
plan card (card 3), and the turn plus CTA on the light card (card 4).

## Slide arc

| Card | Background | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | ink | **Hook.** 4 tilted paper clippings (masthead + date + headline, Georgia 600, 38px); bottom-left `They’re right.` at 120px; `1 / 4` bottom-right. |
| 2 | ink | **The wrong AI.** Eyebrow, display sentence (124px, max 11ch), body 40px. |
| 3 | ink | **The other kind.** Display 88px + body 34px, then the plan card (label · Live · "Your exam is in 9 weeks." · 6 sessions · 9 week-bars · 4 phases). |
| 4 | light | **The turn + CTA.** Eyebrow "Same technology. Opposite goal.", display 96px with the first sentence dimmed, `Build my plan →` button + "Free during early access. No card.", footer with URL. |

Wordmark on cards 2–4 (card 1 has no room and no need). Card counter on all.

## Content needed

- Four verified clippings (masthead, date, exact headline, link).
- Cards 2–4 copy in one language (EN or FR).
- Destination URL for the footer of card 4.

## Production notes

Card 1 clippings are typeset placeholders — the design calls for replacing
them with verified screenshots (masthead + date visible) before publishing.
Test split from the design: full carousel → parents 25–54; cards 2–4 as a
standalone student carousel → 16–18.

## Caption formula

Two or three sentences that agree with the headlines then turn ("Same
technology, opposite goal."), trust line, "Link in bio." No hashtag wall.
